About Us

Shakti Research Centre and Academia

Established in 2024 under the Shakti Groups of Education, SRCAA is a digital academic and research institution founded by Sudhakaran T., a respected lecturer, award-winning researcher, and academic professional. We are accredited by the International Trade Council (ITC) and affiliated with the Global Association of Online Trainers and Examiners (GAOTE).

Our Mission

Fostering an inclusive academic environment where research and education are accessible to all, upholding high standards of academic integrity, promoting global collaboration, and encouraging lifelong learning.

Our Vision

To become a global leader in research and education by connecting educators, researchers, and students worldwide, while continually advancing innovation and excellence.

Our Founder

Sudhakaran T.

Sudhakaran T., the visionary founder of Shakti Research Centre and Academia (SRCAA), is a distinguished academic professional and researcher with a postgraduate degree in Master of Commerce. With extensive experience as a visiting faculty in various colleges, he has significantly contributed to academia through his published research papers in national and international journals.

Driven by a mission to bridge gaps in research-focused institutions, positioning SRCAA as a platform for inclusive, accessible, and globally collaborative education.
